Topic 1.1

Food Variety and Its Sources

Every living thing needs food. Humans eat a huge variety of food because we are omnivores, and almost all of it comes either from plants or from animals.

Topic 1.2

What Do Animals Eat?

Animals are grouped by what they eat: herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat other animals, and omnivores eat both. Some animals also eat dead matter and are called scavengers.
