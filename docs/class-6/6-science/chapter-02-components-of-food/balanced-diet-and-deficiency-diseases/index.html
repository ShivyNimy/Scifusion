Balanced Diet and Deficiency Diseases

A balanced diet contains all nutrients in the right amounts, plus water and roughage. Missing a nutrient for a long time causes a deficiency disease.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Deficiency of vitamin A causes night blindness; vitamin B1 causes beriberi; vitamin C causes scurvy; vitamin D causes rickets; vitamin K affects clotting.
  • Iron deficiency causes anaemia; iodine deficiency causes goitre; calcium and phosphorus deficiency weakens bones and teeth.
  • Protein-energy malnutrition appears as kwashiorkor (swollen belly and legs) and marasmus (extreme thinness).
  • Cooking can destroy vitamin C; boiling vegetables for long removes water-soluble vitamins, so steaming or quick cooking is better.

Worked examples

Example 1 A sailor's diet and scurvy

Sailors on long voyages once ate only dry biscuits and salted meat, and developed bleeding gums and loose teeth — scurvy, caused by lack of vitamin C. Adding lemons and oranges cured them, which is why citrus fruit was carried on ships.

Example 2 Planning a balanced lunch

Chapati (carbohydrate), dal (protein), a green vegetable (vitamins and minerals), a little ghee (fat), a salad (roughage) and a glass of water make a balanced lunch. Only rice and ghee would be energy-rich but nutrient-poor.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Night blindness is caused by the deficiency of:
    A. Vitamin AB. Vitamin CC. IronD. Calcium
    Show answer

    Answer: Vitamin A

    Vitamin A is needed by the retina; carrots, papaya, mango and green leafy vegetables are good sources.

  2. MCQ Goitre is caused by the deficiency of:
    A. IodineB. IronC. Vitamin KD. Protein
    Show answer

    Answer: Iodine

    Iodine is needed to make thyroid hormones; iodised salt prevents goitre.

  3. Short answer What is a balanced diet?
    Show answer

    It supplies energy, building material and protective nutrients in correct proportions, keeping the body healthy.
