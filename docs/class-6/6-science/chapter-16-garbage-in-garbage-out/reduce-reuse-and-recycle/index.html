Reduce, Reuse and Recycle

The best waste is the waste we never create. The 3Rs — reduce, reuse, recycle — cut pollution and save resources.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Reduce: carry a cloth bag, avoid unnecessary packaging, print less.
  • Reuse: refill bottles, use both sides of paper, donate old clothes and books.
  • Recycle: paper, glass, metal and some plastics can be processed into new products.
  • Recycling paper saves trees and water and reduces landfill volume.
  • Plastic takes hundreds of years to decompose and harms animals that swallow it.

Worked examples

Example 1 Recycling paper

Old newspapers are shredded, soaked, pulped and pressed into new sheets. Recycling one tonne of paper saves roughly 17 trees and thousands of litres of water.

Example 2 The cloth bag habit

A single cloth bag reused daily for a year replaces hundreds of thin plastic bags. That is reducing waste at the source instead of cleaning it up later.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The 3Rs of waste management are:
    A. Reduce, Reuse, RecycleB. Read, Run, RestC. Remove, Return, RebuildD. Reduce, Replace, Repair
    Show answer

    Answer: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

    These three strategies cut waste and conserve resources.

  2. MCQ Recycling paper helps mainly by:
    A. Saving trees and waterB. Increasing pollutionC. Making plasticD. Keeping animals
    Show answer

    Answer: Saving trees and water

    Recycling reduces the need to cut trees and saves energy and water.

  3. Short answer Give two ways you can reduce plastic use at home.
    Show answer

    Carry a cloth bag for shopping; refill bottles instead of buying new ones.
