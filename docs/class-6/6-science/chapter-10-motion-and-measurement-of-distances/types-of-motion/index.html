Types of Motion

Motion is a change of position with time. It can be straight-line, circular, periodic, oscillatory or rotational, and the same object can show more than one type.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Rectilinear motion: a car on a straight road.
  • Circular motion: a point on a wheel, the motion of the Moon around Earth.
  • Periodic motion repeats at equal time intervals; oscillatory motion moves to and fro about a mean position.
  • Rotational motion: the spinning of a top about a fixed axis.
  • Objects like a sewing machine needle show oscillatory motion while the wheel shows circular motion.

Worked examples

Example 1 A child on a swing

The swing moves to and fro about its lowest point, so it is oscillatory and also periodic. The path is part of a circle, so it also has a circular aspect.

Example 2 The Moon

The Moon travels around Earth (circular, periodic motion) while also rotating about its own axis. One object, two motions at once.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The motion of a point on a fan blade is:
    A. CircularB. RectilinearC. OscillatoryD. Random
    Show answer

    Answer: Circular

    A point on a rotating blade traces a circle.

  2. MCQ A motion that repeats at equal intervals is called:
    A. PeriodicB. CircularC. RandomD. Uniform
    Show answer

    Answer: Periodic

    Periodic motion returns to the same state again and again.

  3. Short answer Give one example each of rectilinear and oscillatory motion.
    Show answer

    A car on a straight road (rectilinear) and a swinging pendulum (oscillatory).
