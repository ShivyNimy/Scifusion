Excretion and Coordination of Life Processes

Excretion removes wastes; control and coordination keep processes in tune through nerves and hormones.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Kidneys filter blood, producing urine; the nephron is the filtering unit.
  • Excretion in plants uses transpiration and storage in leaves, bark and resins.
  • The neuron conducts impulses; the synapse passes signals between neurons.
  • The brain and spinal cord form the central nervous system; reflex actions protect the body quickly.
  • Hormones such as insulin and thyroxine regulate metabolism; auxins control plant growth.

Worked examples

Example 1 Reflex action

Touching a hot object triggers a reflex arc through the spinal cord, moving the hand away before the pain signal reaches the brain.

Example 2 Auxins and phototropism

Auxin accumulates on the shaded side of a stem, causing cells to elongate, so the shoot bends towards light.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The functional unit of the kidney is the:
    A. NephronB. NeuronC. AlveolusD. Nephridium
    Show answer

    Answer: Nephron

    Each kidney has about a million nephrons.

  2. MCQ Which hormone lowers blood glucose?
    A. InsulinB. GlucagonC. ThyroxineD. Adrenaline
    Show answer

    Answer: Insulin

    Insulin helps cells take up glucose.

  3. Short answer What is a reflex action? Give one example.
    Show answer

    A rapid automatic response to a stimulus, such as withdrawing the hand from a hot object.
