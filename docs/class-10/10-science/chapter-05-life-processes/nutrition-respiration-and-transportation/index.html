Nutrition, Respiration and Transportation

Life processes keep organisms alive: nutrition supplies matter and energy, respiration releases energy and transport moves substances around the body.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Autotrophic nutrition: photosynthesis in plants; heterotrophic: animals and fungi.
  • Digestion breaks food into absorbable molecules; villi absorb nutrients.
  • Aerobic respiration: C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy.
  • Transport in humans: heart, blood vessels and blood; in plants: xylem and phloem.
  • Transpiration pulls water up and cools leaves.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why the heart has four chambers

Separate oxygenated and deoxygenated blood flows allow efficient oxygen delivery to the body, needed by warm-blooded animals with high energy demands.

Example 2 Stomata opening and closing

Guard cells swell with water to open stomata for gas exchange and close them to reduce transpiration when water is scarce.

    Answer: Respiration

  2. MCQ Water and minerals in a plant move through:

    Answer: Xylem

    Xylem carries water and minerals upward.

  3. Short answer Why is the small intestine long?

    Its length and villi greatly increase surface area for absorption.
