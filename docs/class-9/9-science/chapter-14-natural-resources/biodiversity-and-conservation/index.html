Biodiversity and Conservation

Biodiversity includes genetic, species and ecosystem variety. Its loss threatens ecosystem services and human welfare.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Hotspots are regions with high species diversity and high threat.
  • Conservation includes protected areas, seed banks and captive breeding.
  • Ecosystem services include pollination, water purification and climate regulation.
  • Sustainable development meets present needs without compromising future generations.
  • Reduce, reuse and recycle, and use renewable energy.

Worked examples

Example 1 Pollinators and food

Without bees and other pollinators, many fruits and vegetables would fail, showing that biodiversity directly supports food production.

Example 2 Wetlands as filters

Wetlands trap sediment and pollutants and recharge groundwater, providing free water treatment to cities.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The variety of life on Earth is called:
    A. BiodiversityB. BiomassC. BiogeographyD. Biosphere
    Show answer

    Answer: Biodiversity

    Biodiversity is the variety of genes, species and ecosystems.

  2. MCQ Sustainable development means:
    A. Meeting present needs without harming future generationsB. Using all resources nowC. Stopping all industryD. Ignoring pollution
    Show answer

    Answer: Meeting present needs without harming future generations

    It balances present and future needs.

  3. Short answer Give two examples of ecosystem services.
    Show answer

    Pollination of crops and water purification by wetlands.
