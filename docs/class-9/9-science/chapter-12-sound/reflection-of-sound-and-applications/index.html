Reflection of Sound and Applications

Sound reflects like light. Echoes and reverberation are reflection effects; ultrasound is used in medicine and industry.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • An echo is heard when the reflected sound reaches the ear at least 0.1 s after the original.
  • Reverberation is the persistence of sound due to multiple reflections in a hall.
  • Echo ranging: d = v × t ÷ 2 for a round trip.
  • Ultrasound is used in sonography, cleaning and flaw detection.
  • Sound-absorbing materials reduce reverberation in auditoriums.

Worked examples

Example 1 Echo distance

An echo returns after 0.6 s (round trip). With v = 340 m/s, distance = (340 × 0.6) ÷ 2 = 102 m.

Example 2 Sonography

High-frequency ultrasound waves reflect differently from tissues of different density, building an image without radiation.

    Answer: Reflection of sound

    A. UltrasoundB. InfrasoundC. LightD. X-rays

    Answer: Ultrasound

    Sonar sends ultrasound pulses and measures echoes.

  3. Short answer What is reverberation?
