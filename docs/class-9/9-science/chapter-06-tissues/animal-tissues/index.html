Animal Tissues

Animals have four main tissue types: epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Epithelial tissue covers and protects surfaces and may absorb or secrete.
  • Connective tissue includes bone, cartilage, blood and adipose tissue.
  • Muscular tissue is of three types: skeletal (voluntary), smooth (involuntary) and cardiac (involuntary, striated).
  • Nervous tissue is made of neurons that conduct impulses.
  • Blood is a fluid connective tissue with plasma, RBCs, WBCs and platelets.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why cardiac muscle never tires

Cardiac muscle cells are striated but involuntary and have many mitochondria and a rich blood supply, letting them contract continuously without fatigue.

Example 2 Bone and cartilage

Bone is hard and rigid due to calcium salts and supports the body; cartilage is flexible and found in the nose and ears.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which tissue connects muscle to bone?
    A. TendonB. LigamentC. CartilageD. Blood
    Show answer

    Answer: Tendon

    Tendons attach muscle to bone; ligaments join bone to bone.

  2. MCQ Voluntary muscles are found in the:
    A. Arms and legsB. HeartC. StomachD. Intestine
    Show answer

    Answer: Arms and legs

    Skeletal muscles are under conscious control.

  3. Short answer Name the four types of animal tissue.
    Show answer

    Epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous tissue.
