Health, Disease and Causes

Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being. Disease results from pathogens, deficiencies, genetic defects or environmental factors.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Infectious diseases are caused by pathogens and can spread.
  • Non-infectious diseases are caused by deficiency, genetics or environment and do not spread.
  • Pathogens include viruses, bacteria, fungi and protozoa.
  • Disease spreads through air, water, food, contact and vectors.
  • A balanced diet and clean environment support health.

Worked examples

Example 1 Cholera in a village

The bacterium Vibrio cholerae spreads through contaminated water. Providing safe water and sanitation breaks the transmission cycle.

Example 2 Why diabetes is not contagious

Type 2 diabetes is caused by insulin resistance related to genetics and lifestyle, not by a pathogen, so it does not spread.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which is an infectious disease?
    A. MalariaB. DiabetesC. HypertensionD. Scurvy
    Show answer

    Answer: Malaria

    Malaria is caused by a pathogen and is transmitted by mosquitoes.

  2. MCQ Non-infectious diseases are caused by:
    A. Deficiency, genetics or environmentB. Pathogens onlyC. Vectors onlyD. Air only
    Show answer

    Answer: Deficiency, genetics or environment

    They do not involve a transmissible pathogen.

  3. Short answer Define health.
    Show answer

    A state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease.
