Cell Division and the Cell Theory

The cell theory states that all living things are made of cells and new cells arise from existing cells. Cells divide for growth, repair and reproduction.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Schleiden and Schwann proposed the cell theory; Virchow added that cells arise from pre-existing cells.
  • Mitosis produces two identical daughter cells for growth and repair.
  • Meiosis produces four gametes with half the chromosome number.
  • Cell division is controlled; uncontrolled division can lead to cancer.
  • Growth in multicellular organisms happens by increase in cell number and size.

Worked examples

Example 1 Wound healing

Skin cells at the edge of a cut divide by mitosis to fill the gap and restore the barrier.

Example 2 Gamete formation

Meiosis halves the chromosome number so that fertilisation restores the normal diploid number in the offspring.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Cells that are genetically identical to the parent are produced by:
    A. MitosisB. MeiosisC. FertilisationD. Budding
    Show answer

    Answer: Mitosis

    Mitosis produces identical daughter cells.

  2. MCQ Meiosis produces:
    A. Four gametesB. Two identical cellsC. One cellD. Eight cells
    Show answer

    Answer: Four gametes

    Meiosis gives four haploid gametes.

  3. Short answer State the cell theory.
    Show answer

    All organisms are made of cells; the cell is the basic unit of life; all cells arise from pre-existing cells.
