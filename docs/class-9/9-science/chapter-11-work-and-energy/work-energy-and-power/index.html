Work, Energy and Power

Work is done when a force moves an object. Energy is the capacity to do work; power is the rate of doing work.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Work W = F × s × cos θ; the SI unit is the joule (J).
  • Kinetic energy KE = ½mv2; potential energy PE = mgh.
  • Work-energy theorem: net work equals change in kinetic energy.
  • Power P = W/t; the SI unit is the watt (W).
  • 1 kWh = 3.6 × 106 J is the commercial unit of energy.

Worked examples

Example 1 Work done in lifting

Lifting a 2 kg book by 1.5 m: W = mgh = 2 × 9.8 × 1.5 = 29.4 J.

Example 2 Kinetic energy of a car

A 1000 kg car at 20 m/s has KE = ½ × 1000 × 400 = 200,000 J = 200 kJ.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The SI unit of work is the:
    A. JouleB. WattC. NewtonD. Pascal
    Show answer

    Answer: Joule

    Work and energy are measured in joules.

  2. MCQ If speed doubles, kinetic energy becomes:
    A. Four timesB. TwiceC. HalfD. Same
    Show answer

    Answer: Four times

    KE ∝ v2, so doubling speed quadruples kinetic energy.

  3. Short answer A 10 kg object is lifted 2 m. Find the work done (g = 9.8 m/s2).
    Show answer

    W = mgh = 10 × 9.8 × 2 = 196 J.
