Adolescence and Puberty

Adolescence is the period between childhood and adulthood when the body becomes capable of reproduction. Puberty is the set of changes that begin this transition.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Puberty starts between about 10 and 14 years and is controlled by hormones.
  • Secondary sexual characteristics develop: voice change, body hair, breast development, growth spurt.
  • Hormones such as testosterone and oestrogen drive these changes.
  • Adolescence also brings emotional and social changes.
  • A balanced diet, exercise and good hygiene are important during this period.

Worked examples

Example 1 The growth spurt

The body grows rapidly in height during early puberty, often reaching close to adult height by the mid-teens. Growth depends on heredity and nutrition.

Example 2 Why adolescents need more protein and iron

Rapid growth builds new tissue and blood; girls also lose iron during menstruation, so a nutrient-rich diet prevents anaemia.
