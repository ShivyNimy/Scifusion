Reproductive Health and Hormonal Changes

Reproductive health means physical and mental well-being in relation to the reproductive system, including hygiene, safe habits and reliable information.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Girls need to manage menstruation hygienically with clean pads and proper disposal.
  • Adolescents should keep the genital area clean and wear loose cotton clothing.
  • Avoid myths and misinformation; seek reliable information from parents, teachers and health workers.
  • The pituitary gland controls growth and reproductive hormones.
  • Respect and consent are part of healthy relationships.

Worked examples

Example 1 Menstrual hygiene

Changing pads every 4–6 hours, washing the area and disposing of pads properly prevents infections and discomfort.

Example 2 Why sleep matters

During adolescence, growth and hormonal changes are demanding, and 8–9 hours of sleep support growth, memory and mood.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Reproductive health includes:
    A. Physical and mental well-beingB. Only heightC. Only weightD. Only exercise
    Show answer

    Answer: Physical and mental well-being

    It covers overall well-being related to reproduction.

  2. MCQ During menstruation, it is important to:
    A. Practise hygiene with clean padsB. Avoid all waterC. Stop schoolD. Avoid milk
    Show answer

    Answer: Practise hygiene with clean pads

    Cleanliness prevents infection and discomfort.

  3. Short answer Name two habits that support adolescent health.
    Show answer

    Balanced diet and adequate sleep (also exercise and hygiene).
