Lightning and Electric Charge

Lightning is a large electric discharge caused by the separation of charges in clouds. Electric charge can be static or flowing.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Friction between cloud particles separates positive and negative charges.
  • When the potential difference is large enough, charge jumps between cloud and ground or within clouds.
  • Lightning produces thunder by rapidly heating air, which expands and produces sound waves.
  • The electroscope detects charge; like charges repel and unlike charges attract.
  • Lightning conductors (earthing) protect tall buildings.

Worked examples

Example 1 Lightning conductor

A metal rod fixed on a building runs down to the ground. It provides a safe path for charge to reach the earth, protecting the structure.

Example 2 Charged balloon

Rub a balloon on your hair and hold it near small paper pieces; the paper is attracted because the balloon carries a static charge.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Lightning is caused by:
    A. Electric dischargeB. Friction of wind onlyC. SunlightD. Rain only
    Show answer

    Answer: Electric discharge

    Charge separation in clouds leads to a huge discharge.

  2. MCQ A lightning conductor works by:
    A. Earthing the charge safelyB. Attracting rainC. Reflecting lightD. Insulating the building
    Show answer

    Answer: Earthing the charge safely

    It provides a low-resistance path to the ground.

  3. Short answer Why does thunder follow lightning?
    Show answer

    Light reaches us almost instantly, while sound takes about 3 s per km, so thunder is heard later.
