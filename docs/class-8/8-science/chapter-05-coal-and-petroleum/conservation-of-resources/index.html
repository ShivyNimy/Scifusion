Conservation of Resources

Conserving fuels means using less, using efficiently and switching to renewable sources such as solar, wind and hydel power.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Switch off lights and appliances when not needed.
  • Use public transport, cycling and walking; carpool where possible.
  • Use renewable energy: solar panels, wind turbines and hydropower.
  • Recycle metals, paper and plastics to save energy.
  • Plant trees to absorb carbon dioxide.

Worked examples

Example 1 Solar water heater

Black panels absorb sunlight and heat water without burning fuel, reducing electricity or gas use.

Example 2 A community cycle lane

Dedicated cycle lanes encourage short trips by bicycle, reducing fuel use and air pollution.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which energy source is renewable?
    A. Solar energyB. CoalC. PetroleumD. Natural gas
    Show answer

    Answer: Solar energy

    Sunlight is continuously available.

  2. MCQ Recycling aluminium saves mainly:
    A. Energy and raw materialB. Water onlyC. Land onlyD. Labour only
    Show answer

    Answer: Energy and raw material

    Recycling aluminium uses far less energy than extracting it from ore.

  3. Short answer State two ways to reduce consumption of petrol.
    Show answer

    Use public transport and maintain properly inflated tyres (or cycle for short trips).
