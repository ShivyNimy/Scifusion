Sexual Reproduction in Plants

Flowers are the reproductive organs of plants. Pollination transfers pollen to the stigma, then fertilisation produces seeds inside a fruit.

Asexual Reproduction in Plants

Some plants reproduce without seeds from a single parent, producing genetically identical offspring. Vegetative propagation, budding and fragmentation are common.
