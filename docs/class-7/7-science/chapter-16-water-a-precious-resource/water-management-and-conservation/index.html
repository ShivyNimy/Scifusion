Water Management and Conservation

Water must be used sustainably. Rainwater harvesting, drip irrigation and reducing pollution all help.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Rainwater harvesting collects and stores rain or sends it into the ground.
  • Drip irrigation waters roots directly and saves water.
  • Dams store water but displace people and affect rivers.
  • Water pollution from sewage, chemicals and plastic reduces usable water.
  • Traditional water structures such as tanks and bunds recharge groundwater.

Worked examples

Example 1 Rooftop harvesting

Rain from a roof is channelled into a storage tank or recharge pit, saving water and replenishing groundwater.

Example 2 Saving water at home

Fixing leaks, closing taps while brushing and reusing kitchen water for plants make a measurable difference.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Rainwater harvesting mainly helps to:
    A. Recharge groundwaterB. Increase pollutionC. Waste waterD. Lower rainfall
    Show answer

    Answer: Recharge groundwater

    Collected rain is stored or allowed to seep into the ground.

  2. MCQ Drip irrigation saves water because it:
    A. Delivers water directly to rootsB. Floods the fieldC. Sprays everywhereD. Uses seawater
    Show answer

    Answer: Delivers water directly to roots

    Water goes to the root zone with little evaporation.

  3. Short answer State two methods of conserving water.
    Show answer

    Rainwater harvesting and drip irrigation (also fixing leaks and reusing water).
