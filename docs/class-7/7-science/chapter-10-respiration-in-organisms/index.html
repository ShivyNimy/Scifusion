Topic 10.1

Respiration and Breathing

Respiration releases energy from food; breathing brings oxygen in and carbon dioxide out. It happens at the cellular level in all living things.

Topic 10.2

Respiration in Plants and Animals

Different organisms have different respiratory surfaces: gills in fish, spiracles in insects, skin in earthworms, and stomata in plants.
