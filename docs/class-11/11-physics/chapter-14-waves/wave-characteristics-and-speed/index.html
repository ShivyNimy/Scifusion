Wave Characteristics and Speed

Waves transfer energy without transferring matter. A wave has wavelength, frequency, amplitude and speed.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • v = fλ; the speed depends on the medium.
  • Transverse waves have perpendicular particle displacement; longitudinal waves have parallel.
  • Sound is longitudinal; light is transverse electromagnetic.
  • Wave speed on a stretched string v = √(T/μ).
  • The principle of superposition: displacements add algebraically.

Worked examples

Example 1 Speed of a wave on a string

A string with tension 100 N and linear density 0.01 kg/m supports waves at v = √(100/0.01) = 100 m/s.

Example 2 Sound in air

A 440 Hz note in air (v = 340 m/s) has wavelength λ = v/f ≈ 0.77 m.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The relation between speed, frequency and wavelength is:
    A. v = fλB. v = f/λC. v = λ/fD. f = vλ
    Show answer

    Answer: v = fλ

    Wave speed equals frequency times wavelength.

  2. MCQ Sound waves in air are:
    A. LongitudinalB. TransverseC. ElectromagneticD. Standing
    Show answer

    Answer: Longitudinal

    Air particles vibrate along the direction of propagation.

  3. Short answer A wave has frequency 50 Hz and wavelength 4 m. Find its speed.
    Show answer

    v = fλ = 50 × 4 = 200 m/s.
