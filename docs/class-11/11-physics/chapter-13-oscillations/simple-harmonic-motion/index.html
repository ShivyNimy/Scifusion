Simple Harmonic Motion

In SHM the restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed toward the mean position. Displacement, velocity and acceleration vary sinusoidally.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • x = A sin(ωt + φ); v = Aω cos(ωt + φ); a = −ω2x.
  • Angular frequency ω = 2π/T; maximum speed = Aω; maximum acceleration = Aω2.
  • Force F = −kx, with ω = √(k/m).
  • Total energy E = ½kA2 is constant, exchanging between kinetic and potential.
  • The period is independent of amplitude for small oscillations.

Worked examples

Example 1 Spring-mass system

A 0.5 kg mass on a spring of k = 50 N/m oscillates with T = 2π√(m/k) = 2π√0.01 ≈ 0.63 s.

Example 2 Pendulum

A simple pendulum has T = 2π√(l/g); a 1 m pendulum has T ≈ 2.0 s.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The period of a simple pendulum depends on:
    A. Length and gB. MassC. AmplitudeD. Material
    Show answer

    Answer: Length and g

    T = 2π√(l/g).

  2. MCQ At the mean position of SHM, the acceleration is:
    A. ZeroB. MaximumC. Equal to gD. Negative
    Show answer

    Answer: Zero

    Restoring force is zero at the mean position.

  3. Short answer A spring with k = 100 N/m carries a 1 kg mass. Find its period.
    Show answer

    T = 2π√(m/k) = 2π√(1/100) = 0.628 s.
