Water and Soil Pollution

Contaminants in water and soil harm ecosystems and human health. Treatment and responsible practice reduce pollution.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • BOD measures the oxygen demand of organic matter in water; high BOD means pollution.
  • Eutrophication is nutrient enrichment that causes algal blooms and oxygen depletion.
  • Pesticides and heavy metals accumulate in soils and food chains.
  • Industrial effluent must be treated before discharge.
  • Green chemistry aims to reduce hazardous materials from the start.

Worked examples

Example 1 BOD and sewage

Untreated sewage has a high BOD because bacteria consume oxygen to decompose organic matter, leaving less oxygen for fish.

Example 2 Reducing pollution at source

Using biodegradable detergents avoids persistent foams and reduces harm to aquatic life.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ High BOD indicates:
    A. Organic pollutionB. Clean waterC. Low bacteriaD. High oxygen
    Show answer

    Answer: Organic pollution

    More organic matter means more oxygen is consumed by bacteria.

  2. MCQ Eutrophication leads to:
    A. Oxygen depletionB. More oxygenC. Cleaner waterD. Less algae
    Show answer

    Answer: Oxygen depletion

    Algal blooms die and decomposition uses up oxygen.

  3. Short answer What is green chemistry?
    Show answer

    Designing chemical products and processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances.
