Class 11 Biology · Chapter 8 · Topic 8.2

Cell Organelles

Organelles perform specialised tasks: energy conversion, protein synthesis, transport, digestion and storage.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Mitochondria produce ATP and contain their own DNA.
  • Ribosomes synthesise proteins; ER transports and modifies them.
  • Golgi complex packages and exports materials.
  • Lysosomes digest waste; peroxisomes handle oxidative reactions.
  • Plastids carry out photosynthesis and storage in plants.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why mitochondria have folded inner membranes

Cristae increase the surface area for the electron transport chain and ATP synthesis.

Example 2 Ribosome factory

Ribosomes translate mRNA into polypeptide chains, the first step of protein synthesis.

Practice questions

  1. MCQ Which organelle packages and exports proteins?
    A. Golgi complexB. MitochondriaC. RibosomeD. Lysosome
    Show answer

    Answer: Golgi complex

    The Golgi modifies, packages and exports materials.

  2. MCQ Chloroplasts are found in:
    A. Plant cellsB. Animal cellsC. BacteriaD. Viruses
    Show answer

    Answer: Plant cells

    Chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis in plant cells.

  3. Short answer Why are lysosomes called suicidal bags?
    Show answer

    Under stress they may rupture and release digestive enzymes, damaging the cell.
