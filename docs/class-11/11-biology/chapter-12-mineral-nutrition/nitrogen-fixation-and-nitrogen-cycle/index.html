Nitrogen Fixation and Nitrogen Cycle

Plants cannot use atmospheric nitrogen directly. Nitrogen-fixing bacteria convert it into usable compounds.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Nitrogen fixation converts N2 into ammonia.
  • Rhizobium fixes nitrogen in root nodules of legumes.
  • Azotobacter and cyanobacteria also fix nitrogen.
  • Nitrification converts ammonia to nitrite and nitrate.
  • Denitrification returns nitrogen to the atmosphere.

Worked examples

Example 1 Root nodules

Rhizobium in root nodules receives carbon compounds and supplies nitrogen compounds to the plant — a mutualistic symbiosis.

Example 2 Crop rotation with legumes

Growing legumes restores soil nitrogen, reducing the need for nitrogen fertiliser.
