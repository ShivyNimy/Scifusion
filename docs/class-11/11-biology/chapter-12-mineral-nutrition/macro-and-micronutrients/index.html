Macro and Micronutrients

Plants need mineral nutrients in different amounts. Deficiency causes specific symptoms.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Macronutrients: C, H, O, N, P, K, Ca, Mg, S.
  • Micronutrients: Fe, Mn, Cu, Mo, Zn, B, Cl.
  • Nitrogen is needed for proteins and chlorophyll; deficiency causes chlorosis.
  • Nutrients are absorbed from soil solution by roots.
  • Deficiency symptoms appear in specific parts of the plant.

Worked examples

Example 1 Nitrogen deficiency

Leaves turn pale yellow because chlorophyll synthesis is limited and growth slows.

Example 2 Chlorosis versus necrosis

Chlorosis is yellowing from loss of chlorophyll; necrosis is death of tissue from severe deficiency.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which is a macronutrient?
    A. NitrogenB. IronC. ZincD. Copper
    Show answer

    Answer: Nitrogen

    Nitrogen is needed in large amounts.

  2. MCQ Deficiency of nitrogen causes:
    A. ChlorosisB. Necrosis onlyC. Wilting onlyD. Leaf drop only
    Show answer

    Answer: Chlorosis

    Pale yellow leaves indicate nitrogen deficiency.

  3. Short answer Why are micronutrients needed in small amounts?
    Show answer

    They serve as cofactors and catalysts rather than structural building materials.
