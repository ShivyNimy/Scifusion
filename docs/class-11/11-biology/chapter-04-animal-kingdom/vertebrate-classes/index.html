Vertebrate Classes

Vertebrates have a backbone and are divided into fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Fish are aquatic with gills and scales; they include cartilaginous and bony fish.
  • Amphibians live in water and on land and have moist skin.
  • Reptiles have dry scaly skin and lay shelled eggs on land.
  • Birds have feathers, wings, beaks and are endothermic.
  • Mammals have hair, mammary glands and most give birth to live young.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why amphibians depend on water

Their eggs lack shells and their larvae have gills, so breeding requires water or moisture.

Example 2 Bird flight adaptations

Hollow bones, feathers, air sacs and powerful muscles reduce weight and increase oxygen supply.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which group is warm-blooded?
    A. Birds and mammalsB. Fish and amphibiansC. Reptiles onlyD. All vertebrates
    Show answer

    Answer: Birds and mammals

    Birds and mammals maintain constant body temperature.

  2. MCQ Reptiles lay eggs:
    A. On land with leathery shellsB. In water without shellsC. Inside the motherD. Not at all
    Show answer

    Answer: On land with leathery shells

    Shelled eggs protect embryos on dry land.

  3. Short answer Give two distinguishing features of mammals.
    Show answer

    Hair on the body and mammary glands for feeding young.
