Growth Hormones and Regulators

Plant growth is regulated by hormones that promote or inhibit growth and development.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Auxins stimulate cell elongation and control phototropism.
  • Gibberellins promote stem elongation, seed germination and flowering.
  • Cytokinins promote cell division and delay senescence.
  • Abscisic acid inhibits growth and closes stomata under stress.
  • Ethylene promotes ripening and abscission.

Worked examples

Example 1 Auxin in phototropism

Auxin accumulates on the shaded side and elongates those cells, bending the shoot toward light.

Example 2 Ripening fruit

Ethylene released by ripe fruit accelerates ripening of nearby fruit; that is why one ripe banana ripens others in a bag.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which hormone promotes ripening?
    A. EthyleneB. AuxinC. CytokininD. Gibberellin
    Show answer

    Answer: Ethylene

    Ethylene promotes fruit ripening.

  2. MCQ Abscisic acid is mainly:
    A. InhibitoryB. Stimulatory for growthC. A cytokininD. An auxin
    Show answer

    Answer: Inhibitory

    ABA inhibits growth and closes stomata under stress.

  3. Short answer Explain phototropism in terms of auxin.
    Show answer

    Light causes auxin to accumulate on the shaded side, where it stimulates elongation, bending the shoot toward light.
