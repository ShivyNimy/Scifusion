Skeletal System and Joints

The human skeleton supports the body, protects organs and allows movement through joints.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • The adult skeleton has 206 bones.
  • The axial skeleton includes skull, vertebral column and rib cage.
  • The appendicular skeleton includes limbs and girdles.
  • Joints may be fibrous, cartilaginous or synovial.
  • Osteoporosis and arthritis affect bones and joints.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why the skull has sutures

Immovable fibrous joints between skull bones lock them tightly to protect the brain.

Example 2 Knee joint

A synovial hinge joint with cartilage and fluid allows smooth bending and straightening.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The number of bones in an adult human is:
    A. 206B. 300C. 150D. 500
    Show answer

    Answer: 206

    Adults have 206 bones.

  2. MCQ The knee is a:
    A. Synovial hinge jointB. Fibrous jointC. Ball-and-socket jointD. Immovable joint
    Show answer

    Answer: Synovial hinge joint

    The knee allows motion in one plane.

  3. Short answer Differentiate the axial and appendicular skeleton.
    Show answer

    The axial skeleton forms the central axis (skull, spine, ribs); the appendicular skeleton forms the limbs and girdles.
