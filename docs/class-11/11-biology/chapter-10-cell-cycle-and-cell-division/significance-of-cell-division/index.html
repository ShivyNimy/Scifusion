Significance of Cell Division

Cell division maintains body size, repairs damage and enables reproduction. Uncontrolled division causes disease.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Mitosis replaces worn-out cells and heals wounds.
  • In plants, meristems divide throughout life to produce new growth.
  • Meiosis maintains the chromosome number across generations.
  • Apoptosis is programmed cell death, balancing division.
  • Checkpoints ensure damage is repaired before division.

Worked examples

Example 1 Wound healing

Cells around a cut divide by mitosis, filling the gap and restoring the tissue.

Example 2 Programmed cell death

Apoptosis removes damaged or unwanted cells; loss of apoptosis contributes to cancer.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Programmed cell death is called:
    A. ApoptosisB. NecrosisC. MitosisD. Cytokinesis
    Show answer

    Answer: Apoptosis

    Apoptosis is controlled, programmed cell death.

  2. MCQ Which cells divide throughout plant life?
    A. Meristematic cellsB. Mature xylemC. SclerenchymaD. Epidermis
    Show answer

    Answer: Meristematic cells

    Meristems contain actively dividing cells.

  3. Short answer Why are checkpoints important in the cell cycle?
    Show answer

    They verify that DNA is intact and replicated before division, preventing the propagation of errors.
