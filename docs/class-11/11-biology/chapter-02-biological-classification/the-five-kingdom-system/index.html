The Five Kingdom System

Organisms are classified into five kingdoms based on cell type, nutrition and body organisation.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Monera: prokaryotic, includes bacteria and cyanobacteria.
  • Protista: eukaryotic unicellular organisms such as Paramecium and diatoms.
  • Fungi: heterotrophic, saprophytic, cell walls of chitin.
  • Plantae: autotrophic eukaryotes with cell walls of cellulose.
  • Animalia: heterotrophic, multicellular eukaryotes without cell walls.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why fungi are not plants

Fungi lack chlorophyll, have chitinous walls and absorb nutrients from dead or living matter, which is why they form their own kingdom.

Example 2 Kingdom Protista

Amoeba and Paramecium are single eukaryotic cells that carry out all life processes, showing the diversity of protists.

  3. Short answer Name the five kingdoms and one example of each.
