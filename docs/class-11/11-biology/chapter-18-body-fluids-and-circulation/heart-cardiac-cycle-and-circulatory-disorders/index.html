Heart, Cardiac Cycle and Circulatory Disorders

The heart pumps blood through pulmonary and systemic circuits. Its rhythm and output are measurable.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles.
  • The cardiac cycle is one heartbeat with systole and diastole phases.
  • Cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume.
  • Blood pressure is measured as systolic/diastolic.
  • Hypertension, atherosclerosis and heart attack are common disorders.

Worked examples

Example 1 Double circulation

Blood passes through the heart twice per circuit — once to the lungs and once to the body — which improves oxygenation efficiency.

Example 2 Electrocardiogram

The ECG records electrical activity of the heart and helps detect arrhythmias and heart damage.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The normal resting heart rate of an adult is about:
    A. 72 beats per minuteB. 50C. 100D. 120
    Show answer

    Answer: 72 beats per minute

    Typical resting rate is around 70–75 bpm.

  2. MCQ Double circulation means blood passes through the heart:
    A. Twice per complete circuitB. OnceC. Three timesD. Never
    Show answer

    Answer: Twice per complete circuit

    Blood passes through the heart twice per circuit.

  3. Short answer What is cardiac output?
    Show answer

    The volume of blood pumped by the heart per minute, equal to heart rate × stroke volume.
