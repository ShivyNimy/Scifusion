Blood and Its Components

Blood transports substances and defends the body. It contains plasma and formed elements.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Plasma is about 55% of blood and carries proteins, nutrients, hormones and wastes.
  • Red blood cells contain haemoglobin and transport oxygen.
  • White blood cells defend the body against infection.
  • Platelets help in blood clotting.
  • The average adult has about 5 L of blood.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why blood is red

Haemoglobin containing iron binds oxygen, giving blood its red colour.

Example 2 Blood clotting

Platelets and clotting factors convert fibrinogen into a fibrin mesh that seals the wound.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which blood cells transport oxygen?
    A. Red blood cellsB. White blood cellsC. PlateletsD. Plasma
    Show answer

    Answer: Red blood cells

    Haemoglobin in RBCs carries oxygen.

  2. MCQ The percentage of plasma in blood is about:
    A. 55%B. 45%C. 25%D. 75%
    Show answer

    Answer: 55%

    Plasma makes up about 55% of blood volume.

  3. Short answer What is the function of platelets?
    Show answer

    They help form clots and stop bleeding at injury sites.
