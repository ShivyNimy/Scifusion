Class 11 Biology · Chapter 7 · Topic 7.1

Animal Tissues

Animal tissues are epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous. Their structure matches their function.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Epithelium may be simple or compound; it covers, protects and secretes.
  • Connective tissue includes loose, dense, cartilage, bone and blood.
  • Muscle tissue is skeletal, smooth or cardiac.
  • Nervous tissue conducts impulses through neurons.
  • Tissues combine into organs and organs into systems.

Worked examples

Example 1 Tendon versus ligament

Tendons attach muscle to bone and ligaments attach bone to bone; both are dense connective tissue with parallel fibres.

Example 2 Blood as connective tissue

Blood has cells suspended in plasma and connects organs by transporting substances, so it is a fluid connective tissue.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which tissue joins bone to bone?
    A. LigamentB. TendonC. CartilageD. Blood
    Show answer

    Answer: Ligament

    Ligaments connect bones at joints.

  2. MCQ Smooth muscle is found in the:
    A. IntestineB. BicepsC. HeartD. Tendon
    Show answer

    Answer: Intestine

    Smooth muscle lines internal organs and is involuntary.

  3. Short answer Name the four types of animal tissue.
    Show answer

    Epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous.
