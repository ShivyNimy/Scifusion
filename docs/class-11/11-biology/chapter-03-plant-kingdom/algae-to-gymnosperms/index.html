Algae to Gymnosperms

Plants are grouped by vascular tissue, seeds and flowers: algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms and angiosperms.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Algae are mostly aquatic and lack true roots, stems and leaves.
  • Bryophytes (mosses) show the first true multicellular body but need water for reproduction.
  • Pteridophytes (ferns) have vascular tissue and reproduce by spores.
  • Gymnosperms have naked seeds and no fruit; many are evergreen conifers.
  • Angiosperms produce flowers and fruits and dominate modern plants.

Worked examples

Example 1 Fern life cycle

Ferns alternate between a dominant spore-producing plant and a small gametophyte that needs water for fertilisation.

Example 2 Why bryophytes are amphibious

They live on land but need water for sperm to swim to the egg, like amphibians.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which group produces naked seeds?
    A. GymnospermsB. BryophytesC. PteridophytesD. Algae
    Show answer

    Answer: Gymnosperms

    Gymnosperm seeds are not enclosed in fruits.

  2. MCQ Ferns reproduce by:
    A. SporesB. SeedsC. FlowersD. Fragmentation
    Show answer

    Answer: Spores

    Pteridophytes produce spores.

  3. Short answer List the plant groups in evolutionary order.
    Show answer

    Algae -> bryophytes -> pteridophytes -> gymnosperms -> angiosperms.
