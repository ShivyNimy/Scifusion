Important Polymers and Their Uses

Many polymers are used in daily life, but their persistence raises environmental issues.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • PVC, polystyrene, bakelite and Teflon have distinct properties and uses.
  • Bakelite is a thermosetting plastic used in electrical fittings.
  • Teflon is non-stick and chemically inert.
  • Bakelite, melamine and epoxy are thermosetting.
  • Biodegradable polymers and recycling reduce plastic pollution.

Worked examples

Example 1 Teflon in cookware

PTFE's inertness and low friction make it ideal for non-stick surfaces.

Example 2 Recycling PET

PET bottles can be recycled into fibres, saving raw material and energy.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which polymer is used for non-stick cookware?
    A. TeflonB. NylonC. PVCD. Polystyrene
    Show answer

    Answer: Teflon

    PTFE (Teflon) resists sticking and chemicals.

  2. MCQ Bakelite is a:
    A. Thermosetting plasticB. ThermoplasticC. Natural polymerD. Elastomer
    Show answer

    Answer: Thermosetting plastic

    It cannot be remoulded after setting.

  3. Short answer Why is plastic pollution a concern?
    Show answer

    Most plastics are non-biodegradable and persist for a very long time, harming wildlife and ecosystems.
