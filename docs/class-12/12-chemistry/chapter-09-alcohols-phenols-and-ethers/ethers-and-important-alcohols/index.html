Ethers and Important Alcohols

Ethers are relatively unreactive solvents; some alcohols are vital industrial and biological compounds.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Ethers are less soluble in water but dissolve many organic compounds.
  • Williamson synthesis makes ethers from an alkoxide and a haloalkane.
  • Methanol is toxic and causes blindness; it is used as a solvent and fuel additive.
  • Ethanol is produced by fermentation and is used as a fuel and solvent.
  • Phenol and its derivatives are used in disinfectants and pharmaceuticals.

Worked examples

Example 1 Williamson ether synthesis

Sodium ethoxide plus bromoethane gives diethyl ether, a classic ether preparation.

Example 2 Denatured alcohol

Adding methanol to ethanol makes it unfit to drink while keeping it usable as a solvent.
