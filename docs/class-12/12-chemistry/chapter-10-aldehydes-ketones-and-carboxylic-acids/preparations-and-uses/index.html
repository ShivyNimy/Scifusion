Preparations and Uses

Carbonyl compounds are prepared by oxidation of alcohols and are used in many industries.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Primary alcohols oxidise to aldehydes; secondary alcohols to ketones.
  • Formaldehyde is used in plastics and as a preservative.
  • Acetone is an important solvent.
  • Acetic acid is used in food and manufacturing.
  • Grignard reagents react with CO2 to give carboxylic acids.

Worked examples

Example 1 Acetone from propan-2-ol

Oxidising a secondary alcohol gives a ketone; propan-2-ol gives propanone (acetone).

Example 2 Formaldehyde in resins

Formaldehyde polymerises with phenol to make bakelite, a thermosetting plastic.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Oxidation of a secondary alcohol gives a:
    A. KetoneB. AldehydeC. Carboxylic acidD. Ether
    Show answer

    Answer: Ketone

    Secondary alcohols give ketones.

  2. MCQ Formaldehyde is used in:
    A. Plastics and preservationB. Fuels onlyC. Glass onlyD. Fertilisers only
    Show answer

    Answer: Plastics and preservation

    It is a key synthetic intermediate.

  3. Short answer How would you distinguish an aldehyde from a ketone?
    Show answer

    Tollens' or Fehling's test: the aldehyde gives a positive result, the ketone does not.
