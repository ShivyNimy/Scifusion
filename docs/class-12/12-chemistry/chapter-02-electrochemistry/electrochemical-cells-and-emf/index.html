Electrochemical Cells and EMF

Electrochemistry connects chemical reactions and electrical energy. Galvanic cells produce electricity; electrolytic cells consume it.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • In a galvanic cell, oxidation occurs at the anode and reduction at the cathode.
  • E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode.
  • A positive E°cell means the reaction is spontaneous.
  • The Nernst equation gives cell potential at non-standard conditions.
  • A salt bridge maintains electrical neutrality.

Worked examples

Example 1 Daniel cell

Zn|Zn2+ || Cu2+|Cu has E° = 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V, so it is spontaneous.

Example 2 Nernst equation

E = E° − (0.059/n) log Q at 25 °C; increasing product concentration lowers E.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ In a galvanic cell, oxidation occurs at the:
    A. AnodeB. CathodeC. Salt bridgeD. Electrolyte
    Show answer

    Answer: Anode

    Oxidation always happens at the anode.

  2. MCQ A positive standard cell potential means the reaction is:
    A. SpontaneousB. Non-spontaneousC. At equilibriumD. Reversible only
    Show answer

    Answer: Spontaneous

    Positive E° corresponds to negative ΔG.

  3. Short answer Why is a salt bridge used in a galvanic cell?
    Show answer

    It completes the circuit and maintains electrical neutrality in the two half-cells.
