Nucleic Acids, Vitamins and Hormones

Nucleic acids store genetic information, vitamins and hormones regulate metabolism.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • DNA has a double helix with base pairing A–T and G–C.
  • RNA carries and translates genetic information.
  • Vitamins are essential in small amounts; deficiency causes specific diseases.
  • Fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, K; water-soluble B and C.
  • Hormones are chemical messengers secreted by endocrine glands.

Worked examples

Example 1 Base pairing

Adenine pairs with thymine by two hydrogen bonds, guanine with cytosine by three.

Example 2 Vitamin C deficiency

Lack of ascorbic acid causes scurvy, with bleeding gums and poor wound healing.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ DNA bases pair as:
    A. A–T and G–CB. A–G and T–CC. A–C and T–GD. A–U and G–C
    Show answer

    Answer: A–T and G–C

    Adenine pairs with thymine and guanine with cytosine.

  2. MCQ Which vitamin is fat-soluble?
    A. Vitamin AB. Vitamin CC. Vitamin B1D. Vitamin B12
    Show answer

    Answer: Vitamin A

    Vitamins A, D, E and K dissolve in fats.

  3. Short answer Why are vitamins required in small amounts?
    Show answer

    They act as coenzymes and regulators rather than as energy sources.
