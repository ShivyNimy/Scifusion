Population Characteristics and Growth

Populations have size, density, age structure and growth patterns that ecologists model.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Population density = number ÷ area or volume.
  • Exponential growth occurs under unlimited resources: dN/dt = rN.
  • Logistic growth includes carrying capacity K: dN/dt = rN(1 − N/K).
  • Age pyramids show the proportion of young, reproductive and old individuals.
  • Population interactions include competition, predation, parasitism and mutualism.

Worked examples

Example 1 Bacteria in a culture

Under ideal conditions, bacteria double every generation, showing exponential growth until resources run out.

Example 2 Predator-prey cycles

Lynx and hare populations oscillate, with predator numbers lagging behind prey numbers.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Logistic growth accounts for:
    A. Carrying capacityB. Infinite resourcesC. No competitionD. Constant death rate
    Show answer

    Answer: Carrying capacity

    Growth slows as N approaches K.

  2. MCQ Population density is:
    A. Individuals per unit areaB. Total individuals onlyC. Birth rate onlyD. Death rate only
    Show answer

    Answer: Individuals per unit area

    Density is numbers per unit area or volume.

  3. Short answer Differentiate exponential and logistic growth.
    Show answer

    Exponential growth assumes unlimited resources; logistic growth includes a carrying capacity that slows growth.
