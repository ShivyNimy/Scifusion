Transgenic Animals and Ethical Issues

Transgenic animals are used for research, medicine and food, raising ethical concerns.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Transgenic animals carry foreign genes in their genome.
  • They are used to study diseases, produce medicines and improve livestock.
  • Cloning and transgenics require strict ethical review.
  • Biosafety and informed consent are important principles.
  • Benefits must be balanced against animal welfare and environmental risks.

Worked examples

Example 1 Transgenic sheep

Sheep engineered to produce therapeutic proteins in their milk are used to make medicines.

Example 2 Ethical review boards

Research involving animals must be approved by committees that weigh scientific value and welfare.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ A transgenic animal:
    A. Carries a foreign geneB. Is always a cloneC. Cannot reproduceD. Is a plant
    Show answer

    Answer: Carries a foreign gene

    A foreign gene has been inserted into its genome.

  2. MCQ Biosafety guidelines protect:
    A. Human health and the environmentB. Only industry profitC. Only animalsD. Only plants
    Show answer

    Answer: Human health and the environment

    They reduce risks from GM organisms.

  3. Short answer Why is animal welfare a concern in biotechnology?
    Show answer

    Genetic modification and cloning can affect animal health and involve ethical trade-offs.
