Recombinant DNA Technology

Biotechnology uses living systems to make useful products. Recombinant DNA technology joins DNA from different sources.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Restriction enzymes cut DNA at specific sequences.
  • DNA ligase joins DNA fragments.
  • Plasmids act as vectors to carry genes into host cells.
  • The polymerase chain reaction amplifies DNA exponentially.
  • Gel electrophoresis separates DNA fragments by size.

Worked examples

Example 1 Making insulin

The human insulin gene is inserted into a plasmid, put into bacteria, and the bacteria produce human insulin.

Example 2 PCR in diagnostics

PCR amplifies tiny amounts of DNA, so infections and genetic conditions can be detected early.

Practice questions

  1. MCQ Restriction enzymes cut DNA at:
    A. Specific recognition sequencesB. Random pointsC. Only endsD. Only RNA

    Answer: Specific recognition sequences

    They recognise specific base sequences.

  2. MCQ PCR is used to:
    A. Amplify DNAB. Cut DNAC. Join DNA onlyD. Extract proteins
    Show answer

    Answer: Amplify DNA

    Polymerase chain reaction multiplies DNA copies.

  3. Short answer What is a plasmid?
    Show answer

    A small circular DNA molecule used as a vector to carry foreign genes into bacteria.
