Ecological Succession and Ecosystem Services

Ecosystems change over time through succession and provide services essential for humans.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Primary succession begins on bare rock; secondary succession follows disturbance.
  • Pioneer species modify the habitat, enabling later species.
  • Climax communities are relatively stable end stages.
  • Ecosystem services include pollination, water purification and climate regulation.
  • Biodiversity increases ecosystem resilience.

Worked examples

Example 1 Lichens on bare rock

Lichens are pioneer species that break down rock and add organic matter, preparing the way for mosses and grasses.

Example 2 Wetland services

Wetlands filter pollutants, store floodwater and recharge groundwater — free services to society.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Pioneer species in primary succession on rock are:
    A. LichensB. TreesC. GrassesD. Ferns
    Show answer

    Answer: Lichens

    Lichens can colonise bare rock.

  2. MCQ Ecosystem services include:
    A. Pollination and water purificationB. Only foodC. Only timberD. Only minerals
    Show answer

    Answer: Pollination and water purification

    These are benefits ecosystems provide to humans.

  3. Short answer What is ecological succession?
    Show answer

    The gradual, predictable change in community composition over time toward a stable climax.
