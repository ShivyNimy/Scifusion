Energy Flow and Nutrient Cycling

Ecosystems have producers, consumers and decomposers. Energy flows one way; nutrients cycle.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Solar energy is captured by producers through photosynthesis.
  • Only about 10% of energy passes to the next trophic level.
  • Ecological pyramids show numbers, biomass or energy at each level.
  • Nutrients cycle through biogeochemical cycles (carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus).
  • Decomposers return nutrients to the soil.

Worked examples

Example 1 Energy pyramid

A pyramid of energy is always upright because energy decreases at each level.

Example 2 Carbon cycle

Photosynthesis removes CO2 and respiration and combustion return it, cycling carbon through living and non-living reservoirs.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Energy transfer between trophic levels is about:
    A. 10%B. 50%C. 90%D. 100%
    Show answer

    Answer: 10%

    The ten per cent law governs energy flow.

  2. MCQ A pyramid of energy is always:
    A. UprightB. InvertedC. HorizontalD. Variable
    Show answer

    Answer: Upright

    Energy always decreases upward.

  3. Short answer Why is the number of trophic levels limited?
    Show answer

    So little energy remains after several transfers that further levels cannot be supported.
