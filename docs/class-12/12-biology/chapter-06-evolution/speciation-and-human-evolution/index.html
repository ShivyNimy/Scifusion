Class 12 Biology · Chapter 6 · Topic 6.2

Speciation and Human Evolution

Speciation occurs when populations become reproductively isolated. Human evolution is traced through fossil hominids.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Allopatric speciation occurs when populations are geographically separated.
  • Reproductive isolation prevents gene flow between diverging populations.
  • Adaptive radiation produces many species from a common ancestor.
  • Hominid evolution includes Australopithecus, Homo habilis and Homo sapiens.
  • Molecular clocks estimate divergence times.

Worked examples

Example 1 Marsupial radiation

In Australia, marsupials diversified into many forms filling niches occupied elsewhere by placental mammals.

Example 2 Homo sapiens origins

Fossil and genetic evidence places the origin of modern humans in Africa, with later migration across the world.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Reproductive isolation leads to:
    A. SpeciationB. ExtinctionC. Hybrid vigourD. Cloning
    Show answer

    Answer: Speciation

    Isolation stops gene flow and allows divergence.

  2. MCQ Adaptive radiation produces:
    A. Many species from one ancestorB. One species from manyC. ExtinctionD. Cloning
    Show answer

    Answer: Many species from one ancestor

    Many species diverge to fill different niches.

  3. Short answer What is allopatric speciation?
    Show answer

    Speciation caused by geographic separation of populations, leading to reproductive isolation.
