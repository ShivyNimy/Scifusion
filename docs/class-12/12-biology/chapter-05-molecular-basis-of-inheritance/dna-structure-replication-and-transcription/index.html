DNA Structure, Replication and Transcription

DNA stores genetic information. Replication copies it, and transcription produces RNA.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • DNA is a double helix with antiparallel strands and complementary base pairing.
  • Semi-conservative replication produces two molecules, each with one parental strand.
  • Transcription uses one DNA strand as a template for mRNA.
  • In eukaryotes, mRNA is processed (splicing, capping, poly-A tail).
  • The genetic code is triplet, degenerate and nearly universal.

  1. MCQ DNA replication is:
    A. Semi-conservativeB. ConservativeC. DispersiveD. Random
    Show answer

    Answer: Semi-conservative

    Each new DNA molecule keeps one parental strand.

  2. MCQ A codon consists of:
    A. Three nucleotidesB. Two nucleotidesC. One nucleotideD. Four nucleotides
    Show answer

    Answer: Three nucleotides

    Codons are triplets of bases.

  3. Short answer Why is DNA a better genetic material than RNA?
    Show answer

    Its double-stranded structure allows accurate replication and repair, and its stability protects information.
