Microbes in Food, Industry and Medicine

Microorganisms are used to make food, medicines and industrial products and to treat waste.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Fermentation by yeast makes bread, beer and wine; Lactobacillus makes curd and cheese.
  • Antibiotics such as penicillin come from microorganisms.
  • Microbes produce enzymes, organic acids, vitamins and biofuels.
  • Biogas is produced by anaerobic digestion of waste.
  • Vaccines and recombinant proteins are produced using microbes.

Worked examples

Example 1 Curd and cheese

Lactobacillus converts milk sugar to lactic acid, curdling the protein and giving curd its tang.

Example 2 Biogas plant

Methanogens digest cattle dung and produce methane-rich biogas used for cooking.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which microorganism is used in bread making?
    A. YeastB. LactobacillusC. RhizobiumD. Amoeba
    Show answer

    Answer: Yeast

    Yeast ferments sugars and releases CO2, raising the dough.

  2. MCQ Biogas is produced by:
    A. MethanogensB. Nitrifying bacteriaC. VirusesD. Algae only
    Show answer

    Answer: Methanogens

    Methanogenic bacteria generate methane in anaerobic conditions.

  3. Short answer Name two food products made using microorganisms.
    Show answer

    Curd (Lactobacillus) and bread (yeast).
