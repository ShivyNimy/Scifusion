Microbes in Agriculture, Waste Treatment and Biocontrol

Microbes improve soil fertility, break down waste and control pests.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Rhizobium fixes nitrogen in legume roots.
  • Mycorrhiza improves phosphorus uptake in plants.
  • Sewage is treated by aerobic and anaerobic bacteria.
  • Biocontrol agents such as Bacillus thuringiensis and Trichoderma control pests.
  • Biofertilisers reduce dependence on chemical fertilisers.

Worked examples

Example 1 Biofertiliser

Rhizobium inoculants added to legume seeds increase nitrogen fixation and yield.

Example 2 Bt cotton

The bacterium Bacillus thuringiensis provides a gene that protects cotton from bollworms.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Rhizobium helps plants by:
    A. Fixing nitrogenB. Killing insectsC. Producing oxygen onlyD. Absorbing water only
    Show answer

    Answer: Fixing nitrogen

    It fixes atmospheric nitrogen in nodules.

  2. MCQ Which is a biocontrol agent?
    A. TrichodermaB. Nitrogen gasC. PlasticD. Diesel
    Show answer

    Answer: Trichoderma

    Trichoderma is a fungus used against plant pathogens.

  3. Short answer How does sewage treatment use microorganisms?
    Show answer

    Aerobic and anaerobic bacteria decompose organic matter, reducing BOD and producing cleaner water.
