Reproductive Health and Family Planning

Reproductive health means well-being in all aspects of reproduction. Family planning helps couples decide the number and timing of children.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Reproductive health includes safe motherhood, contraception and prevention of STIs.
  • Contraceptive methods include barrier, hormonal, IUD and surgical methods.
  • Condoms also protect against sexually transmitted infections.
  • Awareness and counselling are essential for reproductive health.
  • Lactational amenorrhea is a natural, temporary method of birth control.

Worked examples

Example 1 Why condoms are dual-purpose

They prevent pregnancy and reduce the transmission of infections such as HIV.

Example 2 IUD action

Copper IUDs release copper ions that affect sperm motility and prevent fertilisation.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Which contraceptive method also prevents STIs?
    A. CondomsB. Oral pillsC. IUDsD. Sterilisation
    Show answer

    Answer: Condoms

    Barrier methods block both sperm and pathogens.

  2. MCQ Reproductive health includes:
    A. Physical, mental and social well-beingB. Only physical healthC. Only fertilityD. Only contraception
    Show answer

    Answer: Physical, mental and social well-being

    It is a broad state of well-being related to reproduction.

  3. Short answer Why is counselling important for reproductive health?
    Show answer

    It helps people make informed, safe choices and avoid misinformation and infection.
