Infertility, ART and Prenatal Care

Assisted reproductive technologies help couples with infertility. Prenatal care protects mother and child.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Infertility may be caused by hormonal, anatomical or other factors in either partner.
  • ART includes IVF, ZIFT, GIFT and surrogacy.
  • IVF fertilises eggs outside the body and transfers the embryo to the uterus.
  • Prenatal diagnosis detects genetic disorders but raises ethical issues when misused.
  • Good nutrition, avoiding alcohol and tobacco and regular check-ups improve outcomes.

Worked examples

Example 1 IVF procedure

Eggs are collected, fertilised with sperm in the laboratory, and the resulting embryo is transferred to the uterus.

Example 2 Why folic acid matters

Adequate folic acid before and during early pregnancy reduces the risk of neural tube defects.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ IVF stands for:
    A. In vitro fertilisationB. In vivo fertilisationC. Internal fertilisationD. Incomplete fertilisation
    Show answer

    Answer: In vitro fertilisation

    Fertilisation occurs outside the body in a dish.

  2. MCQ Which is used to detect genetic disorders before birth?
    A. Prenatal diagnosisB. VaccinationC. DialysisD. Biopsy of the mother
    Show answer

    Answer: Prenatal diagnosis

    Prenatal tests examine foetal cells or fluid.

  3. Short answer What is surrogacy?
    Show answer

    An arrangement in which a woman carries and delivers a child for another couple or person.
