Embryonic Development and Parturition

The zygote divides to form a blastocyst, which implants in the uterus. Development proceeds through trimesters until birth.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Cleavage produces a blastocyst that implants in the endometrium.
  • Gastrulation forms the three germ layers.
  • Organogenesis builds organs from the germ layers.
  • Oxytocin triggers uterine contractions during birth.
  • Lactation is stimulated by prolactin.

Worked examples

Example 1 Identical twins

A single zygote splits into two embryos, so identical twins share the same genetic material.

Example 2 Oxytocin in childbirth

Oxytocin causes strong uterine contractions and is also used medically to induce labour.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The zygote implants in the:
    A. UterusB. Fallopian tubeC. OvaryD. Cervix
    Show answer

    Answer: Uterus

    Implantation occurs in the uterine wall.

  2. MCQ Which hormone initiates labour contractions?
    A. OxytocinB. InsulinC. ThyroxineD. Testosterone
    Show answer

    Answer: Oxytocin

    Oxytocin stimulates uterine muscle contraction.

  3. Short answer Why are identical twins genetically the same?
    Show answer

    They develop from a single fertilised egg that splits into two embryos.
