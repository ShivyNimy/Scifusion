Male and Female Reproductive Systems

Human reproduction involves specialised organs and gametes. Hormones control gamete production and the menstrual cycle.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • The testes produce sperm and testosterone; sperm mature in the epididymis.
  • Ovaries produce eggs and oestrogen and progesterone.
  • Fertilisation occurs in the fallopian tube.
  • The menstrual cycle averages 28 days, with ovulation around day 14.
  • The placenta exchanges nutrients, gases and wastes.

Worked examples

Example 1 Hormonal control of the cycle

FSH stimulates follicle growth, LH triggers ovulation, and progesterone maintains the uterine lining.

Example 2 Why the placenta is essential

It links the foetal and maternal circulations without mixing blood, allowing exchange of gases and nutrients.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Fertilisation in humans occurs in the:
    A. Fallopian tubeB. UterusC. OvaryD. Cervix
    Show answer

    Answer: Fallopian tube

    Sperm meets the egg in the fallopian tube.

  2. MCQ Ovulation is triggered by a surge of:
    A. LHB. FSHC. ProgesteroneD. Prolactin
    Show answer

    Answer: LH

    The LH surge induces ovulation.

  3. Short answer What is the function of the corpus luteum?
    Show answer

    It secretes progesterone, which maintains the uterine lining for implantation.
