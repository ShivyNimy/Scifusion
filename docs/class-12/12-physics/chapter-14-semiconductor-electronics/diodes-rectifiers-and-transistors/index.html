Diodes, Rectifiers and Transistors

Semiconductor devices are used in power supply, amplification and digital electronics.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • A half-wave rectifier uses one diode; a full-wave rectifier uses a bridge of four.
  • A Zener diode works in reverse breakdown to stabilise voltage.
  • A transistor has emitter, base and collector; a small base current controls a large collector current.
  • Transistors operate as amplifiers or switches.
  • Logic gates and integrated circuits are built from semiconductors.

Worked examples

Example 1 Full-wave rectifier

Four diodes in a bridge convert both halves of the AC cycle into DC.

Example 2 Transistor as a switch

A small base current turns on a larger collector current, allowing a tiny signal to control a large load.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ A transistor has:
    A. Emitter, base and collectorB. Source, drain and gateC. Anode and cathodeD. Input and output only
    Show answer

    Answer: Emitter, base and collector

    These are the three regions of a bipolar junction transistor.

  2. MCQ A Zener diode is used for:
    A. Voltage regulationB. AmplificationC. RectificationD. Oscillation
    Show answer

    Answer: Voltage regulation

    It stabilises voltage in reverse breakdown.

  3. Short answer Explain why a transistor can amplify a signal.
    Show answer

    A small change in base current produces a much larger change in collector current.
