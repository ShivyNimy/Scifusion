Spectral Series and Lasers

Hydrogen spectra fall into series. Stimulated emission underlies lasers.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Lyman series: transitions to n = 1 (UV).
  • Balmer series: transitions to n = 2 (visible).
  • Paschen, Brackett and Pfund series lie in the infrared.
  • 1/λ = R(1/n12 − 1/n22) gives the Rydberg formula.
  • Lasers use population inversion and stimulated emission to produce coherent light.

Worked examples

Example 1 Balmer line

The n = 3 to 2 transition gives the red H-alpha line at 656 nm.

Example 2 Laser in surgery

Coherent, intense, monochromatic laser light can cut tissue precisely with minimal damage to surrounding areas.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The Balmer series lies mainly in the:
    A. Visible regionB. UltravioletC. InfraredD. Microwave
    Show answer

    Answer: Visible region

    Transitions to n = 2 produce visible lines.

  2. MCQ A laser produces light that is:
    A. CoherentB. IncoherentC. PolychromaticD. Randomly phased
    Show answer

    Answer: Coherent

    Laser light has a constant phase relationship.

  3. Short answer State the Rydberg formula.
    Show answer

    1/λ = R(1/n12 − 1/n22), giving wavelengths of hydrogen spectral lines.
