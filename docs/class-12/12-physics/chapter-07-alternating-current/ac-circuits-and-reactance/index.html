AC Circuits and Reactance

Alternating current varies sinusoidally. Resistors, capacitors and inductors respond differently to AC.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • RMS values: Irms = I0/√2 and Vrms = V0/√2.
  • Inductive reactance XL = ωL; capacitive reactance XC = 1/(ωC).
  • Impedance Z = √(R2 + (XL − XC)2).
  • Resonance occurs when XL = XC at ω = 1/√(LC).
  • Power in AC: P = VrmsIrms cos φ, where cos φ is the power factor.

Worked examples

Example 1 Impedance calculation

With R = 30 Ω, XL = 40 Ω and XC = 0, Z = √(900 + 1600) = 50 Ω, so Irms = 220/50 = 4.4 A.

Example 2 Resonance in a radio

Tuning a capacitor changes the resonant frequency of an LC circuit so it matches the station.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Power factor is:
    A. cos φB. sin φC. tan φD. Z/R
    Show answer

    Answer: cos φ

    The power factor is the cosine of the phase angle.

  2. MCQ At resonance in an LCR series circuit:
    A. Impedance is minimumB. Impedance is maximumC. Current is zeroD. Voltage is zero
    Show answer

    Answer: Impedance is minimum

    XL and XC cancel, leaving only R.

  3. Short answer Calculate the RMS value of a sinusoidal current with peak 10 A.
    Show answer

    Irms = 10/√2 ≈ 7.07 A.
