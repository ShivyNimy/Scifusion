Bar Magnets and Earth's Magnetism

Magnetic materials interact through fields. A bar magnet behaves like a magnetic dipole, and Earth has its own magnetic field.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • A magnetic dipole has a magnetic moment m from south to north pole.
  • Torque τ = mB sin θ; potential energy U = −mB cos θ.
  • Earth's magnetic field has components: declination, dip and horizontal component.
  • Magnetic field lines emerge from the north pole and enter the south pole.
  • Magnetic materials align along field lines.

Worked examples

Example 1 Compass needle

The needle aligns with the horizontal component of Earth's field, pointing toward magnetic north.

Example 2 Magnetic torque

A magnet of moment 0.5 A·m2 in a 0.2 T field at 30° experiences τ = 0.5 × 0.2 × 0.5 = 0.05 N·m.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ The magnetic moment of a magnet is directed from:
    A. South to north poleB. North to south poleC. East to westD. Up to down
    Show answer

    Answer: South to north pole

    The magnetic moment points from the south to the north pole inside the magnet.

  2. MCQ The angle of dip at the magnetic equator is:
    A. 0°B. 90°C. 45°D. 180°
    Show answer

    Answer: 0°

    The field is horizontal at the magnetic equator.

  3. Short answer What is the angle of declination?
    Show answer

    The angle between magnetic north and geographic north at a place.
