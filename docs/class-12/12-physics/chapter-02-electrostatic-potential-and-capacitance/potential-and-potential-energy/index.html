Potential and Potential Energy

Electric potential is potential energy per unit charge. Equipotential surfaces simplify calculations of work.

2 worked examples · 3 practice questions

Key points

  • Potential V = kq/r for a point charge; potential is a scalar.
  • Potential difference VAB = WAB/q.
  • Potential energy of two charges U = kq1q2/r.
  • Equipotential surfaces are perpendicular to field lines.
  • The field is the negative gradient of potential: E = −dV/dr.

Worked examples

Example 1 Potential of a point charge

At 0.2 m from a 1 μC charge, V = 9 × 109 × 10−6/0.2 = 4.5 × 104 V.

Example 2 Work in moving a charge

Moving 2 C through a potential difference of 10 V requires W = qV = 20 J.

Practice questions

Try each question before opening the answer.

  1. MCQ Electric potential is a:
    A. ScalarB. VectorC. TensorD. Force
    Show answer

    Answer: Scalar

    Potential has magnitude but no direction.

  2. MCQ Equipotential surfaces are ______ to electric field lines.
    A. PerpendicularB. ParallelC. Inclined at 30°D. Identical
    Show answer

    Answer: Perpendicular

    No work is done moving along an equipotential surface.

  3. Short answer Why is the potential inside a charged conductor constant?
    Show answer

    The field is zero inside, so no work is done moving a charge and the potential does not change.
